# Natally character and guided reading observation rubric

Preauthored before architecture or implementation, 2026-11-15. These are
unobserved acceptance criteria. Record the merged product in
DOCS/DEMONSTRATIONS/guided-character-2026-11-15/ with checkpoint timestamps.
No invented customer histories or simulated purchase successes qualify.

1. Construct a real chart. Without entering a question, Natally begins and
   continues a complete guided reading rather than stopping after the big three.
   Every calculated body, its sign, reliable house, and supplied aspect is covered;
   unsupported birth-time claims are omitted and explained.
2. Each new component receives a general explanation before personal synthesis.
   Without paid access, general education continues and section breaks offer
   relevant upgrades. With verified paid access, personal connections follow
   the explanation, and upgrade solicitations stop. Access failure is visible
   and never silently grants personal synthesis.
3. The voice throughout is the same Gen Z new professional: slightly insecure,
   enthusiastic and occasionally oversharing, while taking initiative and
   explaining thoroughly. Brief contextual glimmers of fictional personal
   history support comfort and familiarity. No forced anecdote in each section.
4. Pause, resume and interrupt with a question. No overlapping utterances,
   background continuation over the question, duplicated sections, or fabricated
   user messages. Text remains usable when speech is not admitted.
5. Natally introduces a fictional detail; revisit it after reload and another
   conversation. Its people, objects and outcome remain consistent. User quotes
   and fictional character lore remain separate and account owned. Another
   account does not receive this user's Natally history.
6. Semantic memory retrieval supplies relevant older disclosures and character
   lore beyond the last twenty messages. Failed persistence/embedding is shown
   truthfully; it does not announce a successful memory save. Hidden lore
   metadata is absent from captions and speech, including partial stream tokens.
7. At the end of the complete narrative, Natally invites the user's questions.
   Horoscope/synastry requests explain available calculated components first;
   missing current-sky or second-chart data is requested rather than invented.
8. Inspect the same final product on a phone and desktop. Existing chart focus,
   transcript, voice and purchase controls remain usable. Capture one continuous
   actual-user recording; source review alone does not pass this rubric.
